# Asking permission for the Cornilescu Bible (Romanian)

Written 2026-11-07. Lamp needs a Romanian Bible before Romanian can be added. The one Romanian Protestants read is
Dumitru Cornilescu's translation (1921 New Testament, 1924 whole Bible). Its status is not clear enough to use without asking:

- Cornilescu gave the text to the **British and Foreign Bible Society** (Bible Society, UK), which still names itself as the
  copyright holder of the 1924 Bible. In Romania it works through the **Societatea Biblică Interconfesională din România**
  (SBIR, Interconfessional Bible Society of Romania), which holds the copyright of the revised edition (2010/2014).
- Cornilescu died in 1975, so in the EU (life + 70 years) his text is likely protected until the end of 2045.
- eBible.org marks only a **Cyrillic-script** 1924 file as public domain, which is no use for today's readers.

So: write to SBIR, and copy Bible Society UK. Ask which edition they recommend, whether Lamp may include it, the credit line
they want, and if they can send a clean digital file.
