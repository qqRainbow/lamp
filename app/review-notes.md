# Notes for App Review

Apple asked for this on the first submission (2026-10-30, "Information Needed" for a new developer account). Paste the
text below into the reply in App Store Connect **and** into App Review Information → Notes, and keep it up to date for
each new version (1.1 adds Spanish, sermon notes and church links; update points 2–5 when it ships).

---

Hello, and thank you for reviewing Lamp. The screen recording is attached. Here is the information you asked for.

**1. Screen recording**
Attached: recorded on an iPhone running the latest iOS, starting from launching Lamp and showing the main features. Lamp has no accounts, no login, no user-generated content shared with other people, and no paid content or in-app purchases, so there are no such flows to show.

**2. Purpose and audience**
Lamp is a free Bible companion app made for a local church and for anyone who wants to read the Bible every day, in English or French. It helps people build a daily habit of reading Scripture: a verse of the day, verses for how you feel (for example anxious, grateful or lonely), the complete Bible in four public-domain versions with search, side-by-side comparison and read-aloud, reading plans, a daily streak, a Bible quiz, memorization, a private prayer journal, verse pictures to share, eight short lessons for people new to faith, and a kids mode. It is for all ages. It is free, with no ads, no account and no data collection.

**3. How to access the features**
No login, setup or sample files are needed. Open the app and everything is available:
- Today tab: verse of the day, daily streak, shortcuts to every feature; the gear icon (top right) opens Settings.
- Find tab: tap a feeling or type a few words to get matching verses.
- Bible tab: pick a book and chapter; tap a verse to highlight, add a note, save, copy, share as a picture, pray about it, study it (cross-references and dictionary), memorize it, or listen from there. The Listen button reads the chapter aloud.
- Quiz tab: pick a level for a 10-question round; there is also a timed mode, a question of the day, "Who said it?" and "Which book?".
- Journal tab: prayers, saved verses and highlights.
- Settings: language (English/French), Bible version, theme and font, kids mode, daily verse notification and evening reminder (local notifications), and backup/restore.

**4. External services**
None. Lamp does not use any server, data provider, authentication, payment, analytics, advertising or AI service. All content (four Bibles, cross-references, dictionary, fonts) is bundled in the app and works offline. Notifications are local notifications scheduled on the device. Sharing uses the standard iOS share sheet. The only links leave the app to Safari: our privacy policy page and, when someone types words related to self-harm in Find, a helpline directory (findahelpline.com).

**5. Regional differences**
None. The app works the same in all regions. The user can choose English or French at any time.

**6. Third-party material**
Lamp contains no protected third-party material. The Bible texts are public domain: King James Version (1769), Berean Standard Bible, World English Bible and Louis Segond 1910. The cross-references come from OpenBible.info under CC BY (credited in Settings → About Lamp), Easton's Bible Dictionary (1897) is public domain, and the fonts are under the SIL Open Font License. Lamp is not in a regulated industry.

Thank you!
