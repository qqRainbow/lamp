Hello, and thank you for reviewing Lamp.

**What's new in 1.1**
Lamp is now also in Spanish, with the Reina-Valera 1909 Bible. It adds sermon notes in the Journal, and lets someone open a reading plan or a sermon outline that their church shared as a link (Settings → Your church → paste the link). These links are read inside the app; nothing is sent anywhere.

**1. Purpose and audience**
Lamp is a free Bible companion app made for a local church and for anyone who wants to read the Bible every day, in English, French or Spanish. It helps people build a daily habit of reading Scripture: a verse of the day, verses for how you feel (for example anxious, grateful or lonely), the complete Bible in five public-domain versions with search, side-by-side comparison and read-aloud, reading plans, a daily streak, a Bible quiz, memorization, a private prayer journal, sermon notes, verse pictures to share, eight short lessons for people new to faith, and a kids mode. It is for all ages. It is free, with no ads, no account and no data collection.

**2. How to access the features**
No login, setup or sample files are needed. Open the app and everything is available:
- Today tab: verse of the day, daily streak, shortcuts to every feature; the gear icon (top right) opens Settings.
- Find tab: tap a feeling or type a few words to get matching verses.
- Bible tab: pick a book and chapter; tap a verse to highlight, add a note, save, copy, share as a picture, pray about it, study it (cross-references and dictionary), memorize it, or listen from there. The Listen button reads the chapter aloud.
- Quiz tab: pick a level for a 10-question round; there is also a timed mode, a question of the day, "Who said it?" and "Which book?".
- Journal tab: prayers, saved verses, highlights and sermon notes (Sermons → New sermon note).
- Settings: language (English, French, Spanish), Bible version, theme and font, kids mode, daily verse notification and evening reminder (local notifications), Your church (paste a link shared by a church), and backup/restore.

**3. External services**
None. Lamp does not use any server, data provider, authentication, payment, analytics, advertising or AI service. All content (five Bibles, cross-references, dictionary, fonts) is bundled in the app and works offline. Church links carry their plan or outline inside the link itself and are read on the device. Notifications are local notifications scheduled on the device. Sharing uses the standard iOS share sheet. The only links leave the app to Safari: our privacy policy page and, when someone types words related to self-harm in Find, a helpline directory (findahelpline.com).

**4. Regional differences**
None. The app works the same in all regions. The user can choose English, French or Spanish at any time.

**5. Third-party material**
Lamp contains no protected third-party material. The Bible texts are public domain: King James Version (1769), Berean Standard Bible, World English Bible, Louis Segond 1910 and Reina-Valera 1909. The cross-references come from OpenBible.info under CC BY (credited in Settings → About Lamp), Easton's Bible Dictionary (1897) is public domain, and the fonts are under the SIL Open Font License. Lamp is not in a regulated industry.
